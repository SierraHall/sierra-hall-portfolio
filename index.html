The journey · A Title I public school district · 2024–present

The tech isn't broken. The morale is.

The 60-second version

Staff believed technology was failing them. I hand-keyed 523 surveys, spent a month in classrooms, and found the technology was old, not absent: 800 Chromebooks were still in their boxes. I cleaned staff records from 752 to the 484 people actually employed, hosted the district's first in-house PD in five years for 219 staff, and implemented a timeclock with 79% first-year adoption.
